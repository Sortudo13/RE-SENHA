# 🎹 Digital Melancholy: Cyberpunk Music Pack
### Created by Alenia Studios

This is a music asset pack specifically designed for indie games seeking a balance between retro (8-bit) and modern rhythms (Trap/Lo-Fi).

## 🎵 Pack Content
The pack includes 6 high-fidelity .WAV tracks:

1. **Midnight Melancholy**: Ideal for menus or night-themed environments.
2. **Digital Heartbreak**: Emotional melody for cutscenes or dialogue.
3. **Champion Groove**: Upbeat rhythm for shops or minigames.
4. **Neon Nights**: Pure energy for action levels or platformers.
5. **Digital Tears**: A Lo-fi piece for reflective moments or credits.
6. **Cyber Punch**: High-intensity music for combat or chases.

## 🛠 Technical Specifications
* **Format**: .WAV (44100Hz).
* **Looping**: All tracks are designed for seamless looping without abrupt cuts.
* **Impact**: Music designed for immediate impact in short clips and gameplay.

## 🚀 Usage Instructions
If you are using engines like **Ren'Py** or **Godot**, ensure the audio channel is set to "Music" for automatic looping.

> **Note for developers:** If you need to adjust the initial volume via code in Ren'Py, change the following line:
> `define config.default_music_volume = 0.7` (Change 0.7 to your desired value).
